Email Thread Summary — CPS Data Questions & Responses
Date range: ~June 3–4, 2026
Participants: Rohit (THG/you), Iliana Vargas (CPS Director, Planning & Data Management), Lisa Lynn (CPS, enrollment/admissions data)

Rohit asked three questions:
Q1 — School type & zoned school data: Requested data to distinguish school types (selective, zoned, magnet, charter, etc.) and identify each student's zoned school assignment, to enable analysis of how many students attend vs. opt out of their assigned school.
Q2 — Grade 9 application & admissions data: Asked for application data, test scores (SSAT/ISEE), and placement outcomes to improve 9th grade forecasting.
Q3 — Birth rate data for Kindergarten forecasting: Asked for birth data at a geographic level fine enough to support school-level Kindergarten projections.

Iliana's response:

School type/zoned data: Sits in the GIS_CPS database on the 7001 server. The 20th-day student-level file has zoned + attending school info. Separate files indicate attendance boundaries and charter status. She'll point Rohit to the right tables once he has DB access.
Birth data: Illinois Dept of Public Health (IDPH) publishes Chicago-wide birth data — no neighborhood breakdown. Census Table S1301 (fertility at census tract level) exists but her team hasn't used it for this work. She shared a "Chicago Births by Year" link.
Grade 9 admissions: Looped in Lisa Lynn, who manages GoCPS application/admissions data.


Lisa Lynn's response:

CPS has several years of application data for grades K–9. They do not use SSAT or ISEE.
